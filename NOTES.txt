Luxembourg - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-14.

Result: almost nothing. The Direction générale de l'immigration (DGIM, since 2023
part of the Ministère des Affaires intérieures) publishes no procedures manual,
notes de service, instructions or lignes directrices for its own case officers.
The only published instructions are ministerial circulars to the communes, which
handle arrival declarations, registration certificates for EU citizens and entries
in the population register. The immigration-related ones are saved here (see
index.tsv); several reproduce or annex DGIM instructions (e.g. 2025-044 with the
DGIM table of registration cases, 2026-076 on the replacement of the temporary
residence authorisation by a long-stay visa).

Sources checked
- https://maint.gouvernement.lu/fr/circulaires.html - circulars to the communal
  sector, 2023 onwards (441 circulars on all subjects; listing read through the
  page's own search service /ctie/searchresults) - immigration subset selected by
  title/description.
- https://maint.gouvernement.lu/fr/circulaires-old.html - same, 2019-2022
  (about 1,200 PDFs incl. annexes) - immigration subset selected by title.
  Circulars before 2019 are not published.
- https://maint.gouvernement.lu/fr/le-ministere/dgim.html and sub-pages
  (Législations, Traitement des données): legal texts and statistics only; for
  procedures the page refers to guichet.public.lu.
- https://mae.gouvernement.lu (former home of the Direction de l'immigration):
  nothing on immigration any more.
- https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/immigration.html: applicant-facing
  procedure descriptions, forms and explanatory notes - not downloaded.
- Legilux (https://legilux.public.lu, SPARQL endpoint
  https://data.legilux.public.lu/sparqlendpoint): document types "circulaire
  ministérielle" (CMIN) and "instruction ministérielle" (IMIN) searched by title
  for étranger / immigration / séjour / asile / réfugié / protection internationale /
  visa / passeport etc.: no immigration circular or instruction later than the
  19th century is published there.

Left out on purpose (same circular series, not about residence procedure):
calls for integration projects, reception/housing capacity for applicants for
international protection, schooling of Ukrainian children, nationality law
(2023-151, 2025-038), general population-register rules (3819, 2025-016),
Brexit parking cards and the Brexit adjustment reserve.
